Why Learn PGP?

Pretty Good Privacy (PGP) remains one of the most widely interoperable standards for end-to-end encrypted email, file protection, and cryptographic software signing. Whether you are sending a confidential message for the first time or setting up long-term keys in Thunderbird or GnuPG, understanding how public-key cryptography works in practice helps you avoid costly mistakes.

These guides are written for everyday users, developers, journalists, and privacy-conscious readers. Each tutorial pairs clear explanations with practical steps you can test immediately using our free client-side Online PGP tool—where keys and messages stay in your browser.
