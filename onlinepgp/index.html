How to Use Online PGP

Welcome to OnlinePGP.com! Our PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) tool lets you secure your messages and verify their authenticity directly in your web browser.

  1. Generate a New Key Pair: Open the "Generate New PGP Keys Pair" section, enter your name, email address, and a strong passphrase, then click Generate New PGP Keys Pair. Save both keys and never share your private key.
  2. Encrypt a Message: Open "Encrypt & Decrypt PGP Message", paste the recipient's public key, enter your plaintext message, and click Encrypt Text.
  3. Decrypt a Message: Paste your private key, enter your passphrase, paste the PGP-encrypted message, and click Decrypt Text.
  4. Sign and Verify a Message: Use your private key and passphrase to create a clear-signed message, or paste a sender's public key and signed message to verify authenticity.

Security & Privacy Transparency: All cryptographic operations (key generation, encryption, decryption, signing, and verification) run locally in your browser via OpenPGP.js. Your keys, passphrases, and messages are never transmitted to or stored on our server. You can inspect the open-source code on GitHub.
