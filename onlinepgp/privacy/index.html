Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

At OnlinePGP.com, your privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your cryptographic data and how cookies and third-party services are used on this website.

1. Local Processing of PGP Keys & Messages

All PGP operations—key generation, encryption, decryption, signing, and signature verification—are executed 100% locally in your web browser using JavaScript (OpenPGP.js). Your keys, passphrases, and messages are never sent to, logged by, or stored on our servers.

2. Cookies, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense

While your cryptographic inputs stay in your browser, this website uses cookies and similar technologies for traffic measurement and advertising:

  • Google Analytics (G-697PK9P2V4): We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and improve our tools and guides.
  • Google AdSense (ca-pub-7594412359714045): Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

3. GDPR & EEA/UK Consent

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies are denied by default until you explicitly grant consent via our consent banner.

4. CCPA / CPRA Rights (California Residents)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected by third-party advertising partners and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@onlinepgp.com.
